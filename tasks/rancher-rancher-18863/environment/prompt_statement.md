I'm working on the logging utilities in our infrastructure management platform and I need to add the ability to inspect X.509 certificate trust chains. We need to be able to take raw certificate data in encoded byte form, parse it into certificate objects, and then determine whether a given certificate was signed by a particular certificate authority. The logic should correctly handle a self-signed certificate being matched against its own CA (returning true), a certificate signed by a third-party authority being matched against that CA (returning true), and a certificate being checked against an unrelated CA that didn't sign it (returning false). This functionality will be used to detect self-signed certificates in logging backend configurations where they aren't supported alongside client authentication.
