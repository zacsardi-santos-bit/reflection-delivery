I'm working on the Dapr control plane Helm charts and I keep hitting a gap with pod anti-affinity on the placement and scheduler StatefulSets. Right now the scheduler always renders a soft ("preferred") pod anti-affinity policy and there's no way to flip it to a hard ("required") one, and the placement service is worse off since it doesn't support a required policy either and it also ignores the local per-component high-availability override when deciding whether to apply anti-affinity at all. That last bit bites people who enable HA just for placement locally but not through the global HA flag, they don't get the spreading they expect.

What I want is for both the placement and scheduler StatefulSet templates to take a configurable pod anti-affinity policy that's either "preferred" (soft, tries to spread pods across zones but still schedules them together if there's no other option, this is the current behavior and stays the default) or "required" (hard, pods must land on different zones and stay unscheduled if that can't be met, so we get real failure-domain guarantees). For placement specifically it should look at both the global high-availability flag and its own local HA override when deciding whether to emit anti-affinity, and when HA is disabled it shouldn't render any anti-affinity rules whatsoever. The topology key for zone spreading should come from the existing global topology key setting so the default stays zone-based.

Oh and I also need a little test helper that filters the Helm template output down to just the placement StatefulSet, mirroring the one that already exists for the scheduler. Point is soft preferences aren't enough for critical HA workloads, operators sometimes need the hard guarantee that placement and scheduler pods spread across failure domains instead of all piling onto one zone. Default behavior (preferred, zone-based topology key) has to stay identical so existing deployments don't change.
