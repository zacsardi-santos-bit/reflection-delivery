I'm chasing a badge bug in the Brave Rewards browser extension. When someone visits a verified publisher's site and there are pending reward notifications, the badge on the extension icon does the wrong thing, it shows a checkmark symbol with a blue/purple background instead of the actual count of pending notifications. For an unverified publisher with pending notifications it already behaves correctly, showing the numeric count with the orange notification background, so the verification status of the publisher is leaking into logic where it shouldn't. A publisher being verified shouldn't change how pending notifications get counted or displayed at all.

So what I want is for the badge update logic to always render pending notifications as their numeric count with the orange color, regardless of whether the publisher is verified. When there are pending notifications on a verified publisher tab, drop the checkmark and use the count, and swap the blue/purple background for the orange one that matches every other pending notification case.

Oh and there's a related thing while you're in there, the arguments passed to the browser's badge APIs should always include the tab identifier field even when we're not targeting a specific tab. In that no-tab case it should explicitly be passed as absent rather than the field just being omitted from the call entirely.

The reason this matters is users literally can't tell how many pending reward notifications they have when browsing a verified publisher, and the mismatch between verified and unverified handling makes the whole feature feel confusing and unreliable.
