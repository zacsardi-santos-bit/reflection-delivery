I'm poking at our tensor comparison diagnostic (the thing we use to debug numerical drift between model runs) and the stats it reports are leaving me blind in a couple spots. Right now each tensor's summary only shows the signed mean, which is basically useless when a tensor has big positive and negative values that cancel out, you get a mean near zero while the actual element magnitudes are huge. I want it to also report an absolute mean, the mean of the element magnitudes, sitting alongside the regular signed mean so the true scale is always visible no matter the sign balance.

Also the quantile stats are annoying to work with, they're stored as four separate nullable fields for specific percentile levels and there's no median at all. I'd rather collapse those into a single dictionary mapping percentile rank to value, covering the 1st, 5th, 50th, 95th, and 99th percentiles. And when a tensor's too big to compute quantiles efficiently, just leave that dict empty instead of stuffing it with nulls.

Oh and the diff section of the report, when we compare two tensors, has no sense of how the absolute differences are distributed. I want a percentile breakdown there too, of the absolute differences between the two tensors, using those same percentile ranks (1, 5, 50, 95, 99), so I can tell at a glance whether the discrepancies are concentrated in a few extreme elements or spread evenly across the tensor. That distribution matters way more than a single scalar summary when I'm chasing subtle drift.
