I'm hitting this annoying thing in Keras where I pass a bad axis to an op, like axis 10 on a 2D tensor, and instead of blowing up right where I wrote it, everything looks fine until execution and then it falls over somewhere way downstream. Super painful to trace back. I'd love for these ops to check the axis up front while the symbolic graph is being built, so if I give an axis that's out of bounds for the tensor's number of dimensions, I get an error immediately at construction time telling me the axis is out of bounds.

The catch right now is that when you build a computation graph with symbolic tensors and hand in an axis bigger than the tensor's rank, nothing gets raised at all, it just silently slips through and bites you later at runtime, which means waiting through training or inference to find out. So I want validation that fires the moment the op is defined.

This should cover certain activation, normalization, and axis manipulation operations that take an axis parameter. And the error message needs to actually include the specific offending axis value and say that it's out of bounds, so I know exactly which number to fix without guessing. Basically catch it early, name the bad axis, done.
