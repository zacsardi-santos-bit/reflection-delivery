I'm hitting a mess with mid-cycle subscription edits in our billing system and how invoice lines get reconciled. When I change something on a running subscription, say the price of a subscription item, the gathering invoice just keeps the old billing lines instead of replacing them with pro-rated versions for the window the old config was active and adding fresh lines for the updated item starting from the time of the change. And there's a zero case too, where the pro-rated amount comes out to zero (the change landed basically right when the period started), in which case the old lines should just get dropped rather than kept around at zero.

Also the draft invoices are wrong. When there are outstanding drafts covering periods the change touches, they still show stale billing periods and incorrect quantities. I want those updated automatically to match the new subscription state, with usage recalculated for the usage-based lines.

The nasty one is when a change would hit an invoice that's already finalized. We can't rewrite those, so instead of silently ignoring it I want a non-blocking warning attached to that invoice so operators actually see the discrepancy. Concretely the validation issue should carry warning severity (not error), an error code signalling that immutable invoice handling isn't supported, name the subscription sync process as the source component, and point at the specific line that couldn't be updated.

Oh and one more, if a draft ends up with no lines left after a change strips everything out of it, delete it automatically instead of leaving an empty broken invoice hanging around. This all matters because mid-cycle changes happen constantly (upgrades, pricing fixes, phase additions) and without correct reconciliation customers get over or underbilled, and empty drafts just pollute the list.
