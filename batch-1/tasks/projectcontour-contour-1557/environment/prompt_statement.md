I'm adding traffic mirroring (aka shadowing) support to HTTPProxy routes and could use your help wiring it end to end. The idea is that when an operator lists services under a route, they should be able to mark one of those services as a mirror target, so requests matched by that route go to the primary backends like normal for load balancing, but a copy of the traffic also gets forwarded silently to the mirrored service. The mirror's response shouldn't touch the client at all, it's purely for testing, debugging, or analysis, the sort of thing teams want for progressive delivery and canary analysis where you observe how a new service version handles real production traffic before sending live users at it. Right now every service listed under a route is just treated as a primary backend for load balancing and there's no way to say "this one's a shadow target."

So I need two things really. First, the data model change so a service can carry a mirror designation. And second, the translation into the proxy's routing layer so that the mirror traffic policy actually gets applied to the underlying route config. Oh and one constraint: only a single mirror per route is supported, so if more than one service in the same route is marked as a mirror, that route (and its virtual host) should be considered invalid and produce no routing rules at all, don't expose it. A route with exactly one mirrored service should keep producing valid routing config that forwards the mirrored traffic to that designated shadow service. Can you implement it?
