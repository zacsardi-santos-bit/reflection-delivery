I'm working on the OpenShift assisted-service operator and I hit a gap with the image service container: it has no idea what its own externally accessible URL is. Right now the image service StatefulSet gets env vars for reaching other services like the assisted-service, but nothing tells it what scheme and hostname external clients actually use to reach the image service itself. In OpenShift the image service is exposed via a Route resource, and that Route carries the external hostname, so I want the reconciler that creates or updates the image service StatefulSet to read the image service's Route, pull the scheme and hostname off it, and inject those into the container as two env vars, one for the scheme and one for the hostname.

The scheme should always be HTTPS since the Route is secure, and the hostname should match the host configured in the image service's Route. These need to be populated automatically during reconciliation by reading from the Route so they stay in sync with the real external address. Oh and if the route isn't available at reconcile time, don't fail hard, just log a warning and fall back to empty strings for both values.

Point of all this is the image service needs to construct correct self-referential URLs for its own endpoints, like when it redirects or advertises download links to clients, and it can't do that without knowing its own external URL.
