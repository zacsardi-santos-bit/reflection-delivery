I'm working on the CockroachDB integration and I want to expand the metrics we pull from its Prometheus monitoring endpoint. CockroachDB exposes tons of metrics about its background jobs (backups, imports, schema changes, replication, automatic stats collection, TTL cleanup, that kind of thing) but we're only grabbing a tiny fraction right now, so operators have no real way to tell when a job is stuck, failing, or burning unexpected resources. A backup silently dying or a schema migration stalling just goes unnoticed until somebody manually digs in, and I'd like alerting and capacity planning based on job activity instead.

So here's what I need collected. Per job type, the number of jobs currently idle, paused, or running. Also per job type, completion and failure counters, jobs that completed successfully, ones that failed with non-retriable errors, and ones that hit retriable errors, and I want those covered both during normal execution and during failure/cancellation handling. Protected timestamp record metrics per job type too (count, age, and expired records). Plus a handful of changefeed-specific job metrics that belong under the changefeed category but aren't being picked up yet. Then global job registry stuff like total claimed jobs and the job adoption iteration counts per cycle (claimed and resumed). Oh and row-level TTL job metrics, row counts, span counts, and per-operation durations.

The integration keeps metric maps that translate the Prometheus names (underscores) to the Datadog names (dots), so the way to wire all this up is to add the new entries into those maps and the integration picks them up automatically. Just make sure the new metrics land in the right maps with correct name translation, changefeed ones alongside the existing changefeed metrics.
