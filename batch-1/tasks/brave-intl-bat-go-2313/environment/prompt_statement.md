I'm building out mobile in-app purchase support for our Leo premium AI assistant subscription, and right now there's just no bridge between what the app stores hand us and our internal order system. When someone buys through the Android or iOS store and submits their receipt, we can't translate that mobile subscription identifier into the right internal product, build the order, and mark it paid. I need a handful of utility functions to cover the whole flow.

First, mapping the subscription identifier to an internal SKU name, covering the various Android build flavors (release, beta, nightly) plus iOS, for both monthly and yearly billing, and returning a well-defined error for anything unrecognized. The product config itself (pricing, credential durations, issuance settings, Stripe product and price IDs) and the payment processor success/cancel redirect URIs need to vary by deployment environment, so dev/local gets one set, staging another, prod another, and unknown environments fall back to dev defaults.

Then I need receipt request validation that gives clear per-field errors for missing required data or unsupported vendor values. Also a function mapping purchase error states, failed, pending, deferred, and unknown status, to distinct structured error codes an HTTP client can branch on programmatically, with a separate generic code for other validation failures and a 500-level response when the error is unexpectedly nil.

Another one assembles the order metadata for a purchase, capturing the external ID plus the payment processor and vendor labels appropriate for Android vs iOS. And one that builds the full new-order structure from a request and its items, handling the edge cases, a zero-total order gets auto-marked paid, a single-item order inherits that item's location, multi-item orders have no location, and validity duration comes from the first item that specifies one.

Finally the orchestrator that ties it together: resolve the subscription to a product, create the order, mark it paid with the metadata, and propagate errors from each step. Without this layer mobile buyers just can't get provisioned, so it's kind of the foundation everything else sits on.
