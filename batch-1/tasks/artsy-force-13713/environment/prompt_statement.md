I'm building out a new area of the app for supplemental conditions of sale tied to auctions, and right now there's just no place for people to actually browse these legal agreements or open up one to read it. Buyers need to find and read the conditions of sale for whatever auction they're bidding in, and without this those docs are basically inaccessible from the main interface, so I want to fix that.

Two pieces here. First, a main listing page that shows all the sale agreements split across a "Current" tab and a "Past" tab, with Current being the default and showing the active auction agreements. The Past tab is where people browse the historical stuff, and I want those grouped by auction type under labeled headings, so benefit auctions under one heading, commercial partner auctions under a separate one, and Artsy-run auctions under their own. Important bit: a category heading should only show up if there are actually agreements in it, so if a category is empty just hide the whole section, don't render a bare heading with nothing under it.

Each agreement entry needs to show the auction name and its date range. Then there's a detail page for an individual sale agreement that also displays the auction name and the associated date range. Oh and the auction name should be visible to the user on both the listing and the detail page, that's the key thing people are looking for.
