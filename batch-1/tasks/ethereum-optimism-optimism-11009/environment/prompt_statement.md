I'm building out the supervisor service for our optimistic rollup and there's a gap I keep hitting: the supervisor has zero awareness of chain head progression on the L2 chains it's connected to. There's no mechanism at all to subscribe to new block arrivals or to poll for safe and finalized head changes, so nothing downstream can react to chain state moving forward.

I want a head monitoring component that subscribes to real-time unsafe head events from a connected chain and notifies any registered listeners when a new unsafe head shows up. The subscription is the flaky part, so if it ever errors out or drops, the monitor needs to automatically resubscribe on its own without me poking it. On top of the live unsafe stream, it should periodically poll the chain for safe and finalized head changes and notify listeners whenever those move too.

Keep the notifications separate please, unsafe, safe, and finalized each get their own listener path so different parts of the system can react to the right kind of update. Oh and lifecycle matters: it does nothing until it's explicitly started, and stopping it should cleanly tear down all the active subscriptions and polling, no leaks.

This is the foundation for interop consolidation and populating the supervisor databases correctly, so it's gotta know the current head across all three states before any of that downstream processing works.
