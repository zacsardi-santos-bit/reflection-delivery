The bounding box used to position the title needs to include the full extent of all edge routes, not just the shapes themselves. Basically walk the route points of every connection when figuring out the top edge, not just the shape positions.

And this should hold for every layout engine, not just ELK (ELK is the one that surfaced the bug because of how it routes back-edges, but the fix shouldn't be engine-specific). Titles are labels for the whole diagram so they've gotta stay visually separated and readable, not looking broken.
