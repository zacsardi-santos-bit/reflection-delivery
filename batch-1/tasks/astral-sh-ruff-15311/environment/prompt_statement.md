I'm poking at the docstring linter's section detection and hit a real gap. Right now if someone writes a section header in all-lowercase, like "returns:" instead of "Returns:", and it shows up after a blank line in the docstring, the linter just ignores it as a section entirely. It treats it as a subsection, so none of the section-related rules apply. The capitalization warning never fires, and neither do the other section checks like the one about a missing blank line after the last section. So people who write lowercase headers get zero feedback about the capitalization problem because the thing isn't even recognized as a section, and they end up with inconsistently formatted docstrings that silently pass.

What I want is for the linter to treat a known section keyword followed by a colon as a proper section header when it appears after an empty line, whether it's title-cased or all-lowercase. Once it's recognized that way, all the normal section rules should kick in, including flagging that the section name isn't properly capitalized, and catching any missing blank line after the last section. Important bit though, don't break true subsections, the lowercase keywords that appear directly below another section's content (not after an empty line) should keep behaving exactly as they do today.

Oh and I need the snapshot files that hold the expected linter output updated to match, both the new violations reported for the lowercase header and the shifted line numbers that come from adding the new case to the fixture file.
