I'm hitting two annoying problems with the lint rules that flag unnecessary else/elif blocks after early exits (the ones for return, raise, continue, and break in a preceding if branch). First, there's a straight-up crash: if the else block's body starts with a backslash line continuation character, valid but weird Python, the linter panics with an arithmetic overflow instead of just warning me. That kills linting for the whole file, which is brutal. What I want is for it to still emit the diagnostic for that else block, just skip the automatic fix in that case since the backslash makes safe reformatting sketchy and unreliable. So report the warning gracefully, no panic, and don't try to rewrite it.

Second thing, oh and this is the more widespread pain, the auto-fix for this entire family of rules is gated behind preview mode. Most of our projects don't turn on preview, so we get the warnings but the linter can't actually correct them for us. These fixes feel mature enough that they should just work in the default config without opting into anything experimental. Can you promote the auto-fix out of preview so it's on by default for all four rules, and fix the backslash crash too? Both behaviors matter because any codebase with a backslash at the start of an else body can't be linted at all right now, and everyone not on preview is missing a fix that's genuinely ready to ship.
