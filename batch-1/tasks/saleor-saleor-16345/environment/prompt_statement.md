I'm getting wrong voucher discount amounts saved on orders whenever a customer pays with both a voucher and a gift card during checkout. The voucher discount recorded on the order is including the gift card's contribution, so it comes out inflated when it should only reflect what the voucher itself actually knocked off.

It's worst with free-shipping vouchers combined with gift cards. The order's undiscounted total ends up wrong because we're computing it off the post-discount shipping price (zero, after the free-shipping voucher kicks in) instead of the original shipping price, so the voucher discount looks way bigger than it should. This matters because voucher usage tracking and order financial summaries lean on accurate discount breakdowns, and conflating the two discount sources messes up analytics, reporting, and potentially refund math downstream.

What I want is: when an order is completed with a voucher and a gift card both present, the voucher's recorded discount should be exactly the voucher's contribution, with the gift card's portion excluded from the voucher discount record. And the undiscounted total should always use the original pre-voucher shipping price even when a free-shipping voucher was applied. So for a free-shipping voucher the order's shipping price is zero, but the undiscounted total still includes that original shipping price. Oh and gift cards should still get fully consumed with the right gift card usage event recorded.

Can you fix the checkout completion logic so voucher discount amount and undiscounted total come out correct with both a voucher and gift card? It needs to hold for all voucher types, percentage discounts on the whole order, free-shipping, apply-once-per-order, and product-specific vouchers, and it has to apply to both the standard checkout completion flow and the order-from-checkout creation flow.
