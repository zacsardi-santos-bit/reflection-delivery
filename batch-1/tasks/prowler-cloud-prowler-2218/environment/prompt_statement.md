I'm building out some VPC coverage in Prowler and there's a gap around AWS network architecture best practices that I want to close. Right now there's nothing checking whether VPCs are spread across regions for DR, whether subnets inside a VPC actually span multiple AZs, or whether a VPC keeps its public and private subnets separated. I want three new checks for these.

First one, `vpc_different_regions`, should look at all the VPCs and pass when they exist in more than one AWS region, and fail if everything's crammed into a single region (disaster recovery being the whole point). Second, `vpc_subnet_different_az`, should go VPC by VPC and confirm each one has subnets living in at least two different availability zones, reporting pass or fail per VPC for that fault tolerance angle. Third, `vpc_subnet_separate_private_public`, should verify each VPC has both public and private subnets and report accordingly, where public just means the subnet's route table has a route out to an internet gateway and private means it doesn't.

Thing is, the VPC service doesn't collect subnet info yet, so none of this works until I extend it. I need the service to retrieve and store subnets, capturing subnet ID, the VPC ID it belongs to, the CIDR block, the availability zone, and a flag for whether it's public or private (derived from that internet gateway route table check). Once the service has that, the three checks can lean on it. Can you wire up the service enhancement plus all three checks?
